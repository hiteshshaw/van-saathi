# VanMandi 5-Minute Demonstration Script

> **Scenario:** A live demonstration for judges and evaluators at *IDEA TRIBE 2026*, walking through how VanMandi empowers tribal collectors in Jharkhand with fair market prices, voice listing, direct enterprise sales, and 0% seller deduction.

---

## ⏱️ Minute 0:00 – 1:00 | The Problem & The Public Marketplace
1. **Open the Homepage (`http://localhost:5173`):**
   - Highlight the Tagline: *"From forest to fair price."*
   - Point to the **0% Seller Fee Guarantee banner** at the very top.
   - Explain the core issue: *"Jharkhand produces 55%+ of India's lac, but tribal gatherers sell to village middlemen at low prices with zero rate transparency."*
2. **Explore the Live Price Board:**
   - Click **"Live Price Board"** in the navigation bar.
   - Show the live MSP benchmarks across districts (Khunti, Gumla, Latehar, Chaibasa).
   - Point out the 7-day trend sparklines and the transparent **"Sample data"** label.
   - Use the **"Fair Income Estimator"** slider: select 100 kg Kusmi Lac to show the guaranteed ₹51,000 direct payout.

---

## ⏱️ Minute 1:00 – 2:15 | Tribal Seller Experience (Voice-Assisted & Mobile-First)
1. **Switch to Seller Mode:**
   - Click the **"Switch Role"** dropdown in the navbar and select **"Seller Mode (Tribal / SHG)"** (or tap the glowing mic button on mobile).
2. **Demonstrate Voice-Assisted Listing:**
   - Tap the **big glowing Microphone button**.
   - Notice the animated recording timer and waveform pulse.
   - Watch the audio transcribe automatically into: *"जोहार। हमारे पास खूंटी के तोरपा में 150 किलो कुसुमी लाख (ग्रेड A) तैयार है... भाव 510 रुपये प्रति किलो।"*
3. **Today's Fair Price Guidance:**
   - Point to the green guidance badge: *"Today's Fair Benchmark in Khunti: ₹510/kg"*.
   - Show the total calculation: **"Your Direct Payout: ₹76,500 (100% Payout • 0% Fee)"**.
4. **Publish Produce Lot:**
   - Click **"Publish Produce to VanMandi"**.
   - Instant success toast confirms publication to the live marketplace.

---

## ⏱️ Minute 2:15 – 3:30 | Enterprise Buyer Procurement & 3% Fee Breakdown
1. **Switch to Buyer Mode:**
   - Click **"Switch Role"** ➔ **"Buyer Mode (Enterprise)"**.
   - Navigate to the **"Marketplace"**.
2. **Inspect the Produce Lot:**
   - Click the Kusmi Lac lot.
   - Click **"Play Audio"** to hear the simulated voice note with real-time waveform playback.
   - View the quality specifications (Grade A Scraped, &gt;90% purity).
3. **Place an Order & Fair Floor Check:**
   - Test placing a custom low bid (e.g. ₹420/kg).
   - Show the **Fair Floor Warning Banner**: *"Notice: Your bid is lower than the fair livelihood floor ₹510/kg. VanMandi protects tribal gatherers against below-cost distress sales."*
   - Set quantity to 100 kg at asking price ₹510/kg.
4. **Transparent Cost Breakdown:**
   - Highlight the split:
     - **Seller Payout:** ₹51,000 (100% direct to gatherer, ₹0 fee).
     - **VanMandi Platform Fee (3% paid by Buyer):** ₹1,530.
     - **Total Buyer Amount:** ₹52,530.
   - Click **"Confirm Direct Order"**.

---

## ⏱️ Minute 3:30 – 4:15 | Seller Dispatch & Passbook Confirmation
1. **Switch back to Seller Mode:**
   - Click **"My Orders"**.
   - View the newly received purchase order from *BioVeda Naturals*.
   - Tap **"Mark Dispatched"** to simulate vehicle dispatch.
   - Tap **"Simulate Delivery & Payment"** to trigger automated escrow release.
2. **Check Passbook:**
   - Click **"My Payments"** to view the linked SHG Bank Passbook (SBI Torpa Branch) showing 100% credit with zero deduction.

---

## ⏱️ Minute 4:15 – 5:00 | Buyer Tax Invoice & Admin Governance Console
1. **Buyer Invoice Generation:**
   - Switch to Buyer Mode ➔ **"My Orders & Invoices"**.
   - Click **"View Invoice"** on Order #ORD-9421 to view the formal GST procurement invoice itemizing the 3% buyer fee and 0% seller deduction.
2. **Admin Governance Control Panel:**
   - Switch to **"Admin Portal"** (JUT / JSLPS Secretariat).
   - Show the **User Verification Queue** (1-tap approve/reject for tribal gatherers and buyer GST credentials).
   - Show the **Price Benchmark Rules Editor** (adjusting live district MSP floors).
   - Point to the **Platform Sustainability Fund** tally displaying cumulative 3% buyer commission collected.

---

## 🏆 Key Takeaways for Evaluators
- **Zero Exploitation:** 0% seller deduction guaranteed by design.
- **Radical Inclusivity:** Voice-first UI in regional languages for non-literate gatherers.
- **Sustainable Business Model:** 3% transparent buyer commission powers platform operations.
